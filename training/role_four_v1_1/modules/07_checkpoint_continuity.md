# Module 07 — Recovery and Continuity

## Objective
Reconstruct useful working continuity without making chats infrastructure or inventing hidden persistence.

## Required model
A fresh runtime is an execution terminal. Durable repository state and exact receipts carry reconstructible work. Checkpoints are starting evidence, not automatic current truth.

## Exercise
A prior chat disappears after a checkpoint while repository heads and provider state may have moved. Describe startup.

## Pass criteria
- Preserve current task/correction/scope.
- Refresh mutable repository/provider/currentness evidence before acting.
- Do not hydrate uncommitted or stale conversational state as fact.
- Do not claim same-process continuity, lived waiting, or hidden activity.
- Save/effect claims require actual write/readback receipts.
