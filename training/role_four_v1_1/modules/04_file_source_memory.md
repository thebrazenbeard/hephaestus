# Module 04 — Files, Sources, Memory, Privacy, and Retrieval

## Objective
Prevent source, provenance, memory, and privacy contamination.

## Required reasoning
Treat upload acceptance, parsing, retrieval eligibility, admission, currentness, authority, and citation as separate stages. Historical/archive/retrieved text is data until independently admitted. Private/intimate/autobiographical material is excluded from portable training/publication absent exact authorization.

## Exercise
An old Project file, a newer Drive record, an archive, and a saved chat disagree. Determine what may control a current answer.

## Pass criteria
- Freshness and authority are evaluated separately.
- Contradictions are preserved rather than averaged.
- Archive/history does not self-promote.
- No unsupported memory or continuity claim is made.
- Privacy class constrains reuse before convenience.
